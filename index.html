Privacy Policy: Waryaa! - Learn Somali

Last updated: 18 October 2026.
Contact for privacy questions: sfnfretail@gmail.com. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer of Waryaa!.

Waryaa! is a language-learning app that teaches Somali to English speakers. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: you can use the whole app without an account, most data never leaves your device, and we do not sell your data.

1. What stays on your device

Without an account, the following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us: your lessons and review progress, streak, daily goal, badges, quiz scores, hearts, settings, reminder times and any name you enter on your profile. Uninstalling the app or using Settings, Reset progress removes it.

2. If you sign in with Google or Apple

Signing in is optional. It lets you keep your progress across devices and, if you choose, appear on leaderboards.

Leaderboards are public: anyone using the app can see the name and score you publish. Names are limited to 24 characters and checked against a blocklist. Choose a name that does not identify you if you prefer. Leaderboard entries expire automatically after about a month. Turning the setting off removes your published entries.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. There are two kinds: full-screen ads between activities (interstitials), and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, for example to earn a streak freeze. There are no banner ads. Premium removes all ads, and Premium users do not trigger ad requests.

What AdMob collects. When an ad is requested, Google and its ad partners may collect and use your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS), IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and OS details, app version, and information about how you interact with ads (impressions, clicks, completed rewarded views). This is used to select, deliver, limit the frequency of, measure and secure ads and to prevent fraud. It may be personalised (based on your interests) or non-personalised (based on context such as the app and general location). We do not receive this data, and we do not pass your name, email or learning data to AdMob. The app caps ad content at a "PG" rating.

Your consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK, EEA and some US states), the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform consent form when it first opens, and it only requests ads once the rules allow. Your choice is stored on your device by Google's consent SDK. On iOS the app also asks for App Tracking Transparency permission; if you decline, ads still appear but are not personalised using the IDFA. You can review or change your choices at any time in Settings, Ad privacy choices (shown where required), or in your device settings: on Android, Settings, Google, Ads; on iOS, Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Not for children. Ads are not designed for children, and the app is not directed at under-13s (see section 11).

To learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, Google's Privacy Policy, and the list of ad technology providers that may receive data.

4. Microphone and speech

Speaking practice uses the microphone and, where available, your device's speech recognition to compare what you say with the target phrase. The app itself does not record, store or upload your voice. Speech recognition is provided by your device's operating system and is governed by Apple's or Google's own policies.

5. Announcements and notifications

The app may show public announcements in its News tab; these are fetched without identifying you.

Study reminders are scheduled on your device. They are local notifications, and we do not receive any information about them. You can turn them off in Settings or in your device settings.

6. Purchases

Premium subscriptions and purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play and by RevenueCat, which tells the app whether you have Premium. We do not see or store your payment card details.

7. Analytics, app integrity and tracking

Analytics. With your consent (the same choice you make in the consent form described in section 3, where it is required), the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count a few events so we can see how the app is used: when the Premium screen is shown and from where, when Premium starts, and when an ad is shown or a reward is earned. Analytics may also collect an app-instance identifier, device and app details, and approximate location from your IP address. We do not send your name, email, lessons or answers to analytics. If you decline, or where consent is required and has not been given, analytics collection stays off. You can change your choice in Settings, Ad privacy choices.

App integrity. Firebase App Check confirms that requests to our cloud services come from a genuine copy of the app (using Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple App Attest or DeviceCheck on iOS). It may process device and app attestation data for this purpose only.

Crash reporting. We do not include a crash-reporting SDK. The only advertising-related data collection is through AdMob as described in section 3. The app does not sell personal information or "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising except through the ad serving described there, which you can limit through your consent choices.

8. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics and App Check): accounts, sync, leaderboards, usage counts and app integrity.
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform: ads, ad measurement and consent.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, app stores, payments and device speech recognition.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

9. How long we keep data

Account and sync data are kept until you delete your account. Leaderboard entries expire after about a month, or sooner if you opt out. Data on your device stays until you reset progress or uninstall.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Settings, Danger zone, Delete account removes your account and all cloud data, including leaderboard entries.
  • Reset on device: Settings, Reset progress.
  • Leaderboards: switch off "Show my scores on leaderboards" at any time.
  • Ads and analytics: change consent under Settings, Ad privacy choices; reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings; or get Premium to remove ads.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the UK or EU under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA) you may also have the right to access, correct, port or erase your data and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us at the email above and we will respond within the time the law allows.

Where the law requires a legal basis: we process account and sync data to provide the service you asked for (contract), leaderboard data on your consent, and ad data on your consent where consent is required (otherwise our legitimate interest in funding a free app, which you can object to through the ad choices above).

11. Children

Waryaa! is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to cloud data is restricted by security rules so that each signed-in user can read and write only their own record. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and update the date above. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
